Somnex respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

## Information We Collect

When you contact us through our website, we may collect your name, email address, and any message content you provide. We may also collect technical information such as browser type and page usage data to improve the website experience.

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to respond to inquiries, improve our services, and maintain the security of our website. We do not sell personal information to third parties(nor do we really know how to).

## Data Retention

We retain contact information only as long as needed to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by law.

## Your Choices

You may contact us at any time to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Please use the contact form on this website to do so.
